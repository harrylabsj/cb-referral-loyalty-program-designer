## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Referral incentives, sweepstakes, privacy practices, advertising claims, and reward rules may be regulated differently by market. <br>
Mitigation: Validate program mechanics with qualified local legal, privacy, tax, advertising, and compliance professionals before launch. <br>
Risk: Generated cultural and market assumptions may be incomplete or out of date for a specific country, channel, or customer segment. <br>
Mitigation: Treat market adaptation notes as hypotheses and verify them with current local research, customer interviews, and reliable sources. <br>
Risk: Reward economics or fraud controls may be insufficient for a specific product, margin profile, or abuse pattern. <br>
Mitigation: Review margins, eligibility rules, redemption limits, tracking logic, and fraud monitoring checkpoints before implementation. <br>
